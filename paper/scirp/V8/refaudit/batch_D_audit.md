## joachims1998text_check

**Confirmed.** Joachims, "Text Categorization with Support Vector Machines: Learning with Many Relevant Features" (ECML 1998, LNCS 1398, pp. 137–142, DOI 10.1007/BFb0026683) uses Ohsumed as its second test collection. From the text of the author's PDF (https://www.cs.cornell.edu/people/tj/publications/joachims_98a.pdf):

> "The second test collection is taken from the Ohsumed corpus compiled by William Hersh. From the 50216 documents in 1991 which have abstracts, the first 10000 are used for training and the second 10000 are used for testing. The classification task considered here is to assign the documents to one or multiple categories of the 23 MeSH 'diseases' categories. A document belongs to a category if it is indexed with at least one indexing term from that category."

Further details: the training corpus has 15,561 distinct terms after preprocessing, and the paper notes "All Ohsumed categories are linearly separable". It is multi-label. The other collection is the Reuters-21578 ModApte split (9,603 train / 3,299 test, 90 categories). Cite Hersh et al. 1994 for the corpus and Joachims 1998 for the 23-category, 10k/10k task setup.

## guo2025deepseek_check

**Confirmed: SFT only, from Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B.**

- **Training: SFT only, no RL.** arXiv v1 §2.4 and v2: "For distilled models, we apply only SFT and do not include an RL stage, even though incorporating RL could substantially boost model performance." The base model is fine-tuned for 2–3 epochs on the ~800k-sample dataset (v2, Appendix B.4.3).
- **Training data.** The data is not purely R1 output. v1 §2.3.3 describes it as about **600k reasoning samples** made by rejection sampling from the RL checkpoint, plus about **200k non-reasoning samples** that reuse parts of the DeepSeek-V3 SFT data (some with CoT generated by DeepSeek-V3). The paper itself calls it "800k samples curated with DeepSeek-R1" (v1) or "800,000 samples generated with DeepSeek-R1" (v2), so "SFT on DeepSeek-R1-curated data" is the most accurate wording.
- **Base model: Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B.** Stated in v1 §2.4 ("The base models we use here are Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, Qwen2.5-Math-7B, Qwen2.5-14B, Qwen2.5-32B, Llama-3.1-8B, and Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct") and in v2 Table 6 (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B: base Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, initial LR 1e-4). The HF model card agrees.
- **Peer-reviewed version: yes.** Guo, D. et al. (DeepSeek-AI), "DeepSeek-R1 incentivizes reasoning in LLMs through reinforcement learning", *Nature* 645(8081):633–638 (published online 17 Sept 2025), **DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09422-z** (Crossref: 194 authors, Daya Guo first). arXiv:2501.12948's journal-ref points to it. The distillation details above appear in arXiv v2 (Jan 2026), which matches the Nature version with its supplementary material. Nature's own page could not be fetched (login redirect), so I did not check its supplementary material directly.
